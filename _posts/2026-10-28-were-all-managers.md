### AI coding is exhausting.

I keep thinking back to [this video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e-pFrQ_Rh0s) by Matt Pocock. He describes it well - it used to be the case that as software engineers, there were parts of our work that involved just getting in there and churning out code. **AI has taken that from us.**

What's left when we can't be the coders? We are forced to think at a higher, strategic level.

### We're all managers.

We're all having to learn the skills usually reserved for the *leaders* of the team - communication, delegation, playing to strengths, coordinating with other humans.

It's like each of us is a solo team, in a world of our own, separated from the code by a layer of our personal individual contributors.

### These skills are not easy

"Not easy" in the sense that it's so darn taxing! No matter how fast the agents go, there's a limit on how fast I, the *human*, can process. So my hours are usually spent on endless missions of *understanding the state of the codebase*, figuring out what to do next, what to tell the team.

Yes, it can be exhilerating to hit that AI code slot machine and get results fast. But at the end of the day, managing this shockingly intelligent yet shockingly disappointing (at times) team can leave me quite burnt out 😪.
